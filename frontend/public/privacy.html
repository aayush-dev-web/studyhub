Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This is a hackathon project. Here's what StudyHub stores and why.

1. Account data

Your name, email, role (student/teacher), and institution, so login and the right dashboard work.

2. Your content

Notes, questions, calendar events, and messages you create, so the features that show them back to you (and, where relevant, your class) work.

3. Who sees what

Your teacher can see your class activity. Other students can see what you post in shared spaces like Community. Nothing is sold or shared outside StudyHub.

4. Questions

This is a student project — reach out to the person who built it for anything specific.
